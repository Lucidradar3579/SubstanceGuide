Fabric Texturing in Substance 3D Painter

Difficulty: Beginner to Intermediate Time: 30-50 minutes Software: Substance 3D Painter Roblox relevance: Essential

Fabric covers clothing, banners, upholstery, backpacks, and soft accessories. Distinguish materials through weave direction, pile, fold scale, and roughness rather than relying on color alone.

Roblox workflow note

Fabric is dielectric: set metalness to 0. Use subtle normal or height weave detail, scaled to the UVs. Roblox clothing workflows may use templates or layered clothing rather than a standard mesh texture, so choose the delivery method for the asset first.

Material Properties

Weave

Directional pattern identifies plain weave, twill, knit, or canvas.

Roughness

Usually moderate to high; satin is smoother and more reflective.

Fold and wear

Folds follow tension and gravity; seams and contact areas concentrate wear.

Starting Reference Values

Use these as practical starting points, then tune the result under the target lighting and at the final in-game viewing distance.

MaterialColor directionRoughnessMetalnessTypical use
Cotton / canvasNatural or dyed color0.50-0.750.0Clothing, tents, bags
DenimUsually indigo or dyed0.45-0.700.0Pants, jackets, upholstery
Wool / fleeceOften low-saturation0.65-0.850.0Coats, blankets, soft props
SatinAny dyed color0.20-0.400.0Ribbons and formal clothing

Roughness values are approximate authoring ranges, not universal physical measurements. Keep metalness at 0 for non-metallic surfaces.

Painter Workflow

1

Choose construction

Decide if the surface is woven, knitted, brushed, or smooth before adding detail.

2

Align the pattern

Set the weave direction to follow garment panels and UV islands. Check seams for scale changes.

3

Shape folds and seams

Use broad tonal and roughness changes at folds, hems, stitches, and stress points.

4

Reduce for Roblox scale

Keep the weave subtle enough that it reads as fabric in-game rather than a repeating grid.

Export and Quality Check

  • Keep base color in sRGB; treat roughness, metalness, and normal maps as linear data.
  • Use the Roblox SurfaceAppearance export preset or confirm each channel is packed and named as expected.
  • Check UV seams, texture scale, compression, and the material under more than one lighting condition.
  • Import into Roblox Studio and compare at the actual camera distance before increasing texture detail.
