Wood Texturing in Substance 3D Painter

Difficulty: Beginner to Intermediate Time: 30-50 minutes Software: Substance 3D Painter Roblox relevance: High

Wood is common in furniture, buildings, tools, doors, and environment props. Grain direction, species color, cut, and finish should all agree with the shape of the object.

Roblox workflow note

Wood is a dielectric material with metalness 0. Align grain with the long axis of boards and beams. Use broad variation for stylized Roblox assets and keep fine pores and scratches restrained.

Material Properties

Grain direction

Follows the board length; end grain differs from side grain.

Finish

Raw, painted, stained, varnished, and weathered wood require different roughness.

Natural variation

Knots, rings, pores, and color shifts should be sparse and scale-appropriate.

Starting Reference Values

Use these as practical starting points, then tune the result under the target lighting and at the final in-game viewing distance.

MaterialColor directionRoughnessMetalnessTypical use
PinePale yellow to warm tan0.45-0.700.0Frames and simple furniture
OakWarm tan to medium brown0.35-0.600.0Furniture and flooring
WalnutMedium to deep brown0.30-0.550.0Dark furniture and trim
Painted woodAny paint color0.35-0.700.0Doors, toys, and props

Roughness values are approximate authoring ranges, not universal physical measurements. Keep metalness at 0 for non-metallic surfaces.

Painter Workflow

1

Choose cut and species

Pick the wood type and whether the visible surface is plank, end grain, veneer, or painted board.

2

Direct the grain

Use triplanar or UV projection deliberately; rotate and mask the pattern so it follows each piece.

3

Build the finish

Layer stain, varnish, edge wear, and dirt according to use. Keep metalness at zero.

4

Inspect joins

Check UV seams and intersections so grain direction and scale remain believable across boards.

Export and Quality Check

  • Keep base color in sRGB; treat roughness, metalness, and normal maps as linear data.
  • Use the Roblox SurfaceAppearance export preset or confirm each channel is packed and named as expected.
  • Check UV seams, texture scale, compression, and the material under more than one lighting condition.
  • Import into Roblox Studio and compare at the actual camera distance before increasing texture detail.
