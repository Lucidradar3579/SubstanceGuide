Plastic Texturing in Substance 3D Painter

Difficulty: Beginner to Intermediate Time: 25-45 minutes Software: Substance 3D Painter Roblox relevance: High

Plastic appears in toys, accessories, electronics, vehicle parts, and props. Its finish ranges from matte molded parts to glossy polished shells, but ordinary plastic remains a dielectric material.

Roblox workflow note

Keep metalness at 0. Vary roughness and normal detail to distinguish hard ABS, soft rubber, and glossy molded plastic. Use subtle mold seams and handling wear rather than broad dirt overlays.

Material Properties

Metalness

Use 0.0 for plastic; colored or glossy plastic is not metal.

Roughness

Lower values sharpen highlights; higher values soften the reflection.

Mold detail

Subtle seams, parting lines, micro-scratches, and stress marks describe manufacture.

Starting Reference Values

Use these as practical starting points, then tune the result under the target lighting and at the final in-game viewing distance.

MaterialColor directionRoughnessMetalnessTypical use
Matte ABSAny opaque color0.35-0.550.0Toys, cases, props
Glossy ABSAny opaque color0.15-0.300.0Polished shells and trim
PolypropyleneOften slightly warm0.35-0.550.0Containers and flexible parts
Rubberized plasticUsually dark or saturated0.50-0.750.0Grips, tires, soft-touch parts

Roughness values are approximate authoring ranges, not universal physical measurements. Keep metalness at 0 for non-metallic surfaces.

Painter Workflow

1

Block in color and finish

Choose the plastic family and set a consistent base color with metalness at zero.

2

Add manufactured details

Place parting lines and injection marks where they make sense for the model, not uniformly across every surface.

3

Vary roughness

Use fingerprints, polishing, or scuffs as small roughness changes. Avoid excessive height that makes the plastic look like stone.

4

Review at asset scale

Inspect the material at the Roblox camera distance and reduce details that collapse into noise.

Export and Quality Check

  • Keep base color in sRGB; treat roughness, metalness, and normal maps as linear data.
  • Use the Roblox SurfaceAppearance export preset or confirm each channel is packed and named as expected.
  • Check UV seams, texture scale, compression, and the material under more than one lighting condition.
  • Import into Roblox Studio and compare at the actual camera distance before increasing texture detail.
