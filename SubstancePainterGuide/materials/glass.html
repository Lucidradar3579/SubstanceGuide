Glass Materials for Roblox in Substance 3D Painter

Difficulty: Intermediate Time: 25-40 minutes Software: Substance 3D Painter Roblox relevance: High

Glass is used for windows, bottles, lenses, display cases, and effects. In Roblox, transparency and reflections are primarily configured on the part or mesh and through scene lighting, not created by a base-color texture alone.

Roblox workflow note

SurfaceAppearance maps cover color, metalness, normal, and roughness; they are not a transmission/refraction setup. Configure transparency on the Roblox object or use the Glass material where appropriate, then use textures for tint, dirt, and surface detail.

Material Properties

Transparency

Set in the Roblox object/material workflow, not painted as ordinary base color.

Roughness

Low for clean glass; higher for frosted or etched surfaces.

Edge cues

Bevels, reflections, tint, and controlled dirt help thin glass read in-game.

Starting Reference Values

Use these as practical starting points, then tune the result under the target lighting and at the final in-game viewing distance.

MaterialColor directionRoughnessMetalnessTypical use
Clear windowNear white with subtle tint0.05-0.200.0Windows and clean panels
Tinted glassSubtle blue, green, or smoke tint0.05-0.250.0Vehicle glass and displays
Frosted glassSoft neutral tint0.35-0.650.0Privacy panels and lamps
Dirty glassSubtle color variation0.15-0.450.0Abandoned or used environments

Roughness values are approximate authoring ranges, not universal physical measurements. Keep metalness at 0 for non-metallic surfaces.

Painter Workflow

1

Set Roblox transparency first

Choose the Roblox part transparency or Glass material behavior before authoring texture detail.

2

Keep the texture subtle

Paint fingerprints, dust, tint, and edge grime on separate layers; avoid dark opaque base colors.

3

Model edge cues

Use bevels and thickness where practical. Thin flat planes need careful lighting and framing to read as glass.

4

Check overlap and sorting

Test multiple transparent objects in Roblox Studio because transparency ordering and reflections can differ from Painter.

Export and Quality Check

  • Keep base color in sRGB; treat roughness, metalness, and normal maps as linear data.
  • Use the Roblox SurfaceAppearance export preset or confirm each channel is packed and named as expected.
  • Check UV seams, texture scale, compression, and the material under more than one lighting condition.
  • Import into Roblox Studio and compare at the actual camera distance before increasing texture detail.
