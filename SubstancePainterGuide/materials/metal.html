Metal Texturing in Substance 3D Painter

Difficulty: Beginner to Advanced Time: 30-90 minutes Software: Substance 3D Painter Roblox relevance: Essential

Build readable metal surfaces for Roblox weapons, armor, machinery, vehicles, and props. The key is to separate bare metal from paint, dirt, and oxidation instead of making every layer metallic.

Roblox workflow note

Set metalness to 1 for exposed metal and 0 for paint, rust, dirt, or other non-metal layers. Roblox reflections depend on scene lighting and environment settings, so judge the exported maps in Studio as well as Painter.

Material Properties

Metalness

Bare metal is 1.0; paint and corrosion are dielectric at 0.0.

Roughness

Polished surfaces are lower; brushed, worn, or oxidized surfaces are higher.

Surface detail

Use directional scratches, edge wear, dents, and restrained oxidation.

Starting Reference Values

Use these as practical starting points, then tune the result under the target lighting and at the final in-game viewing distance.

MaterialColor directionRoughnessMetalnessTypical use
Brushed steelGray0.25-0.451.0Tools, armor, machinery
Polished aluminumLight gray0.10-0.251.0Trim and clean hardware
CopperWarm reddish brown0.18-0.351.0Pipes, fittings, decorative pieces
Painted steelChosen paint color0.30-0.550.0 paint / 1.0 exposed metalVehicles and props

Roughness values are approximate authoring ranges, not universal physical measurements. Keep metalness at 0 for non-metallic surfaces.

Painter Workflow

1

Set the material regions

Use polygon fill, material IDs, or masks to separate exposed metal from paint and grime.

2

Establish the finish

Choose the metal tint and roughness first. Keep scratches aligned with the manufacturing process, such as brushing or machining.

3

Layer wear deliberately

Add edge wear and oxidation with masks. Keep corrosion localized and ensure its metalness is zero.

4

Check under Roblox lighting

Export the SurfaceAppearance maps and inspect reflections and color in Studio before adding more detail.

Export and Quality Check

  • Keep base color in sRGB; treat roughness, metalness, and normal maps as linear data.
  • Use the Roblox SurfaceAppearance export preset or confirm each channel is packed and named as expected.
  • Check UV seams, texture scale, compression, and the material under more than one lighting condition.
  • Import into Roblox Studio and compare at the actual camera distance before increasing texture detail.
