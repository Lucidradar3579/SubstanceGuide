Stone Texturing in Substance 3D Painter

Difficulty: Beginner to Intermediate Time: 30-50 minutes Software: Substance 3D Painter Roblox relevance: High

Stone materials suit architecture, ruins, statues, cliffs, and props. Surface scale and breakup should match the type of stone and how close the player can get to it.

Roblox workflow note

Stone is dielectric with metalness 0. For Roblox, use silhouettes and broad color variation to carry the material at distance, then add restrained roughness and normal detail for close views.

Material Properties

Formation

Granular, crystalline, layered, or veined patterns depend on the stone type.

Roughness

Usually moderate to high, with polished stone as a deliberate exception.

Weathering

Chips, cracks, stains, and moss gather in plausible locations rather than uniform noise.

Starting Reference Values

Use these as practical starting points, then tune the result under the target lighting and at the final in-game viewing distance.

MaterialColor directionRoughnessMetalnessTypical use
LimestoneWarm gray to pale cream0.55-0.800.0Walls, carvings, ruins
SandstoneTan, ochre, or red-brown0.60-0.850.0Cliffs, blocks, monuments
GraniteCool or warm gray with flecks0.45-0.700.0Paving and structural props
MarbleLight with restrained veins0.20-0.450.0Polished floors and statues

Roughness values are approximate authoring ranges, not universal physical measurements. Keep metalness at 0 for non-metallic surfaces.

Painter Workflow

1

Choose stone type and scale

Set the grain or vein size according to the mesh dimensions and intended camera distance.

2

Layer color and roughness

Use broad mineral variation first, then add subtle fine grain and roughness breakup.

3

Add localized weathering

Place cracks, chips, dirt, and moss where water, contact, and edges make them plausible.

4

Check tiling and distance

Review repeated patterns on large surfaces and reduce small detail if it shimmers or muddies at game scale.

Export and Quality Check

  • Keep base color in sRGB; treat roughness, metalness, and normal maps as linear data.
  • Use the Roblox SurfaceAppearance export preset or confirm each channel is packed and named as expected.
  • Check UV seams, texture scale, compression, and the material under more than one lighting condition.
  • Import into Roblox Studio and compare at the actual camera distance before increasing texture detail.
