Leather Texturing in Substance 3D Painter

Difficulty: Intermediate Time: 30-50 minutes Software: Substance 3D Painter Roblox relevance: High

Leather is useful for bags, belts, seats, gloves, armor details, and fashion accessories. Grain size, finish, and wear should match the scale and intended quality of the object.

Roblox workflow note

Keep leather dielectric with metalness 0. Use controlled roughness variation for polished contact areas and finer normal detail for grain. On small Roblox assets, broad color and shape cues matter more than tiny pores.

Material Properties

Grain

Fine and irregular for smooth leather; larger, softer marks for suede.

Roughness

Moderate for finished leather and higher for suede or worn untreated surfaces.

Wear

Creases and polished edges follow bends, seams, and frequent contact.

Starting Reference Values

Use these as practical starting points, then tune the result under the target lighting and at the final in-game viewing distance.

MaterialColor directionRoughnessMetalnessTypical use
Full-grain leatherTan to deep brown0.35-0.550.0Premium bags and seats
Aniline leatherRich natural dye0.30-0.500.0Soft, natural finish
SuedeMuted warm colors0.60-0.800.0Soft accessories and clothing
Patent leatherOften deep saturated color0.10-0.250.0Glossy fashion pieces

Roughness values are approximate authoring ranges, not universal physical measurements. Keep metalness at 0 for non-metallic surfaces.

Painter Workflow

1

Set the hide tone

Start with the main dye color and keep variation broad rather than speckled.

2

Add grain at the right scale

Use a fine grain pattern for leather and check it on the final UV scale to avoid visible tiling.

3

Place folds and wear

Mask creases along bends and seams. Add gentle polishing on high-contact edges.

4

Protect the silhouette

On small accessories, prioritize color blocking, seam placement, and edge highlights over pore detail.

Export and Quality Check

  • Keep base color in sRGB; treat roughness, metalness, and normal maps as linear data.
  • Use the Roblox SurfaceAppearance export preset or confirm each channel is packed and named as expected.
  • Check UV seams, texture scale, compression, and the material under more than one lighting condition.
  • Import into Roblox Studio and compare at the actual camera distance before increasing texture detail.
