Material Library for Roblox Assets

Scope: Common PBR materials Use: Starting points for Painter projects

Browse the material guides for practical setup notes, Roblox considerations, and roughness and metalness starting ranges. Always judge the final look in Roblox Studio under the lighting used by your experience.

Shared PBR baseline

Most non-metal materials use metalness 0. Bare, exposed metal uses metalness 1. Base color is authored as sRGB; roughness, metalness, and normal data are linear. Values are starting ranges, not universal constants.

Material Guides

Metal

Separate bare metal from paint, oxidation, and dirt. Use roughness to control reflection sharpness.

Plastic

Use dielectric values, molded details, and finish variation for hard or soft plastics.

Leather

Build grain, folds, polished contact edges, and wear at an appropriate asset scale.

Fabric

Distinguish weave, knit, pile, seams, and folds while keeping texture readable in-game.

Wood

Align grain with boards and beams; vary species, cut, finish, and weathering.

Skin

Match the character workflow and use restrained color and roughness variation.

Glass

Configure transparency in Roblox and use texture maps for tint, dirt, and surface cues.

Stone

Match grain scale and weathering to the stone type and camera distance.
